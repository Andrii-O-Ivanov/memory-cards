# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** February 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Memory Cards" game collects, uses, and protects your data. This project is created for educational purposes.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
This application is a client-side React application. We respect your privacy and strive to collect as little data as possible:
* **Local Storage:** We use your browser's `localStorage` to save your game progress, including your score, time, and number of moves for the Leaderboard. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any external servers.
* **Cookies:** We use a single cookie to remember your consent to this privacy policy in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). 

## 2. Third-Party Services
* **The Rick and Morty API:** The game fetches character images and basic data from the public Rick and Morty API. This API does not track your personal information through our app.
* **GitHub Pages:** The game is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect standard, anonymized server connection logs (like IP addresses) for security and performance metrics.

## 3. GDPR Compliance and Your Rights
Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, modify, or delete your data. Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. 
You can delete all your data at any time by:
1. Clearing your browser's cookies.
2. Clearing your browser's local storage data for this website.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the project, please refer to the author mentioned in the `README.md` file.
